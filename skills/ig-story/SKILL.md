# ig-story

Stories aren't a mini feed. The feed is how strangers find you. Stories are
where people who already follow you decide whether you're someone worth buying
from, and they're the only place on Instagram where one tap becomes a
conversation.

Only followers see them, so the job is totally different: depth, not reach.

## The daily shape

Three to seven frames a day. Past about seven, people start tapping through
faster and the last frames play to an empty room, which is exactly where most
people put their ask.

```
1       THE OPEN     something happening today, with a face or a hand in shot.
2-3     THE MIDDLE   the real content: the process, the result, the mistake.
4       THE ASK      one sticker. Poll, question box, quiz or link.
5       THE CLOSE    the answer, the result, or a setup for tomorrow.
```

Put the ask on frame 4, not frame 7. Viewers drop off with every tap, and the
ask should reach the ones still watching.

## Stickers, and what each one is really for

| sticker | job | use when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Poll** | the easiest tap there is | you want lots of responses, not information |
| **Question box** | collecting the exact words people use | you need content ideas or objections, word for word |
| **Quiz** | teaching by letting people get it wrong | there's a common misconception in the niche |
| **Slider** | a mood check | nothing important. Fun, low value |
| **Link** | the only clickable spot apart from the bio | there's somewhere real to send them |
| **Countdown** | a deadline people can follow | a launch, a live, a closing date |
| **Add yours** | occasional reach beyond followers | the prompt is something anyone in the niche can answer |

The question box is the most underrated one here. Every answer is a caption, a
Reel hook or a DM opener in the audience's own words. Pass them to `/ig-reel` as
formula #16.

## Rules

- **A face or a hand in frame one.** Text on a coloured background gets tapped
  past, and the first frame decides whether anyone sees the rest.
- **One idea per frame.** Nobody reads a paragraph on a story.
- **Keep text in the middle.** On 1080x1920, nothing above y=250 or below
  y=1600. The profile row sits at the top and the reply bar at the bottom.
- **Talk to one person.** "You", singular. Stories are the closest thing to a
  DM that isn't one.
- **Don't reshare your own feed post with no comment.** It's the most skipped
  frame on the platform. If you're pointing at a post, say what happened in the
  comments and why it's worth going back for.
- **Sell in stories, not in posts.** Three frames of context, one of offer, one
  of proof. That sells more than a feed post on the same thing and costs you no
  reach.

## The DM funnel, done honestly

What works: a story that names a problem, a question box or poll that lets
people say "that's me", then a reply to everyone who answered. The conversation
starts because they spoke first.

Automated keyword DM replies are a real, supported feature for professional
accounts, through Instagram's own tools and approved partners. Mass-DMing people
who never interacted isn't, and it's what gets accounts restricted. Simple rule:
**they act first, then you reply.**

## Output

The frames in order, each with what's on screen, what's said and which sticker,
plus what to do with the responses:

```
STORIES  ·  Tuesday  ·  5 frames

1  [selfie, walking]      "Third refund request this year. Same reason every time."
2  [screen recording]     the clause, highlighted
3  [talking]              "Approval is a feeling. Delivery is a date."
4  [poll]                 "Ever been burned by a late approval?"  Yes / Not yet
5  [text on photo]        "Reply and I'll send you the clause."

After: reply to everyone who votes Yes. That's the whole funnel.
```

Nothing is posted. The user posts it.
