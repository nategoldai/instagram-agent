# voice.md

Copy this to `~/.claude/instagram/voice.md` and fill it in. Every skill in the
pack reads it. Ten minutes here is the difference between scripts you film and
scripts you rewrite.

Don't want to fill it in by hand? Send Claude three of your own reels or
captions and say "write my voice.md from these". That beats guessing at the
answers.

This file matters more on Instagram than anywhere else, because you have to say
the words out loud. A line you'd never say isn't a draft, it's a retake.
